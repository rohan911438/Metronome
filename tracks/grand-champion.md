# Grand Champion — $25,000

Picked from **all tracks**. There's no separate application: every submission is considered.

Source: https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis

## Eligibility
- Submit a valid project to any track (for us, Track 01)

## What we do to stand a chance
The Grand Champion is usually the project that looks most like a **real startup that could only exist on Monad**. So we:
- Pitch Metronome as **infrastructure** ("fair execution for all of Monad"), not just an app
- Show a path after the hackathon: a router for aggregators and other dApps, more markets, and fees for keepers and the protocol
- Make the demo, README and architecture diagram the most polished thing the judges see
- Show numbers: gas per order, gas per settle, parallelism benchmark, and victim loss vs. AMM
